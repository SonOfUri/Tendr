---
title: Payment links
description: Share a link or a QR code and get paid, with no website or code on your side.
---

A payment link is a reusable page at `https://usetendr.tech/pay/<your-store>/<link>`. The payer opens it,
sees what they are paying for, and presses **Pay**. Tendr then opens the normal checkout, so everything
else works as it does for a session your own site creates: quotes, gasless payments, refunds, webhooks
and payouts to your account. Tendr never holds the money.

Make links with the API (`POST /v1/payment_links`, see the [reference](/api-reference/payment-links)),
the [TypeScript SDK](/sdks/typescript#payment-links) or the dashboard.

## Kinds of link

| Kind | Payer sees | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `fixed` | The price you set. | An invoice, a product, a deposit. |
| `open` | A box to type an amount. | Donations, tips, "pay what you owe". |

An open link can set `min_amount` to raise its floor. It cannot lower it.

## Currencies and the $1 minimum

A link is priced in `USD` or in an ISO 4217 currency Tendr has a rate for (for example `EUR`, `NGN`,
`JPY`). The order is converted to USD when the payer presses Pay, and the currency's own decimals apply
(`JPY` has none).

Every link takes at least **$1.00 USD equivalent**, checked on the link amount before fees:

- A fixed amount is checked when you create or edit the link, and again when the payer presses Pay,
  because rates move. A link priced near the floor can start refusing payments later.
- An open amount is checked when the payer presses Pay. The page shows the effective minimum: the larger
  of your `min_amount` and the $1.00 floor in the link's currency.
- Below the minimum the API answers `400 AMOUNT_TOO_LOW` with `details.min_amount`.
- When a non-USD rate is not available the API answers `503 FX_RATE_UNAVAILABLE`. Try again shortly.

## Who pays the fees

`fee_payer` and `gas_payer` are optional on a link. Leave them unset (`null`) and the link follows your
store defaults. Set them and they apply to every payment through that link. `gas_max_usd` caps the
network fee Tendr will sponsor for the link. A session's own setting still wins when your code creates
the session. See [gasless payments](/gasless/overview) and the [gas policy](/gasless/gas-policy).

## The payer's note

With `note: "optional"` (the default) the payer can leave a short message with the payment. Set
`note: "off"` to hide the box.

- At most 280 characters of plain text. Control characters are removed and whitespace is collapsed.
- It is stored on the session and returned as `payer_note` on every session event (see
  [webhooks](/api-reference/webhooks#payment-link-fields)). It is always shown as text and links in it
  are never clickable.
- The email you get for a payment names the link and quotes the note.

## Test mode

Test and live links are separate. A link made with a test key has a `url` that ends in `?mode=test`.
Without `?mode=test` only live links resolve, so a test link never answers on the live address. Test
links use test checkout: no real money moves.

## QR codes and preview cards

Each link has a QR code at its `qr_url`: `/pay/<store>/<link>/qr` (PNG, 1024 px). Add `format=svg` for
SVG. It encodes the link's address, including `?mode=test` for a test link.

Pasting the link into a chat or a social post shows a 1200 x 630 preview card with your store name, the
title, the price and your logo. The logo appears only when Tendr hosts it; otherwise the card shows your
store's initial.

## Switching off, archiving, limits

| Action | How | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Switch off | `PATCH` with `status: "inactive"` | The page says the link is not taking payments. Switch it back on any time. |
| Archive | `DELETE`, or `PATCH` with `archived: true` | Hidden from the default list and unavailable. The payer sees it as switched off. `archived: false` restores it. |
| Expire | `expires_at` | After this time the link is `expired`. |
| Limit | `max_uses` | The link stops once that many payments have settled. |

A link is never deleted: its payments and totals stay. The `slug` is part of the shared address and
**cannot be changed** after creation. To retire an address, archive the link and make a new one.

`PATCH` accepts `null` to clear `expires_at`, `max_uses`, `description`, `min_amount`, `fee_payer`,
`gas_payer` and `gas_max_usd`. Omit a key to leave it as it is. Unknown keys are rejected.

### How `max_uses` works

`max_uses` is safe for single-use invoices. Every **open session reserves one use**, so two payers
cannot both start paying a link that has one use left. A session waiting for its deposit holds the use
until it expires. Once the deposit has arrived, the session holds the use until the payment settles or
fails, even if that runs past the session's expiry.

- While a payer is mid-payment on the last use, a second payer gets `409 LINK_RESERVED`: "Someone is
  paying this link right now, try again shortly." If that session expires without a deposit, the use
  is free again.
- When every use has settled, new payers get `409 LINK_UNAVAILABLE` with `availability: "used_up"`.
- A session started from the page lives 5 minutes until the payer gets a quote, then the normal session
  lifetime. A payer who walks away frees the use after those 5 minutes.
- **Late deposits** (money that arrives after the session is marked expired) are refunded and never
  count as a use.
- **Refunds do not free a use.** A refunded payment stays used.
- `use_count` can exceed `max_uses` in one narrow case: a session that is past its expiry but not yet
  swept receives a deposit that settles.

A link accepts at most 200 open sessions that have a quote. Past that the page answers
`429 LINK_BUSY`. Sessions that have no quote yet do not count: they expire within minutes.

## Totals

Every link read returns:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `use_count` | Settled payments. |
| `total_paid`, `total_refunded` | In the link's currency. |
| `total_paid_usd`, `total_refunded_usd` | In USD, as charged. |

Only settled payments count towards `total_paid`. A refund counts towards `total_refunded` once it has
executed on chain. To list the payments of one link, use `GET /v1/payments?payment_link_id=<id>`.

## Who cannot use links

A store that settles into its own contract (an integrator with payout mode `wallet`) takes payments
through [hooks](/integrations/hooks) instead. Creating a link for it returns `409 LINK_NOT_SUPPORTED`.

## What the payer sees

1. Your store name and logo, the title, the description, and the price (or an amount box).
2. If the link is not available, a short reason (switched off, expired, used up) and no Pay button.
3. A note box when `note` is `optional`.
4. **Pay** creates the session and sends the payer to the hosted checkout to choose a token and send.
5. After payment, the success screen links back to the payment link page.

Nothing is created until the payer presses Pay: opening or previewing a link makes no session.
