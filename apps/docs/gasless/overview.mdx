---
title: 'Gasless payments'
description: 'Understand optional sponsorship and token-paid network fees.'
---

Gasless routes let eligible wallets authorize a token payment while a relayer or paymaster submits the transaction. Availability depends on the deployment, chain, token, wallet and provider configuration. Do not promise gasless payment for every supported asset.

Tidepool Test mode uses normal wallet transfers with customer-paid test network fees. Gasless signing and merchant sponsorship are unavailable in Test mode, even when store sponsorship preferences are saved.

## Who pays

A session may set `gas_payer` to `merchant` or `shopper`. Otherwise the payment link or store default applies.

- Merchant sponsorship follows the store's enabled policy, allowed chains/assets, per-payment cap and monthly budget.
- Shopper-paid gasless routes can collect the network fee in the payment token.
- A normal wallet transfer requires the wallet's native network fee.

The checkout displays the applicable quote and fee before authorization. Copying a deposit address and sending from another app is a normal transfer; store sponsorship does not apply to that external send.

## Limits and fallback

Live gasless execution has its own deployment switch in addition to ordinary live payments. Missing providers, unsupported token methods, exhausted budgets or wallet limitations can make a route unavailable. Use returned capabilities and quotes rather than fixed chain or dollar-cost assumptions.

EVM authorization/permit routes, supported smart-wallet paymasters, Solana paymasters, Tron forwarders and NEAR delegate actions have different prerequisites. A supported chain does not imply every wallet on it supports the required signing method.

## Verification

Relayer submission or deposit detection does **not** mean the order is paid. Wait for settlement and verified `payment.succeeded`, just as for a normal payment.

Session/payment `network_fee` records who paid, the fee mode, token/base-unit amount, USD value, method and fallback reason when available. See [Gas policy](/gasless/gas-policy) and [Webhooks](/api-reference/webhooks).
