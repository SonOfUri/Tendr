---
title: Gas policy and usage
description: Set how much gas your store sponsors, read the month's usage, and list sponsored transactions.
---

The gas policy decides when Tendr pays the network fee for your shoppers. There is one policy per mode
(test and live). See [gasless payments](/gasless/overview) for how sponsorship works.

## Endpoints

| Endpoint | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `GET /v1/gas-policy` | The mode's policy. Defaults (disabled) while none is set. |
| `PUT /v1/gas-policy` | Change any subset of the fields. |
| `GET /v1/gas-policy/usage?month=YYYY-MM` | Budget use for a month. Defaults to the current month. |
| `GET /v1/gas-sponsorships` | Every sponsored or relayed transaction, newest first. |

Each call acts on the mode of the key or dashboard session that makes it.

### Who can call

- Any secret key can read the policy and usage, including keys made by a connected site such as the
  WooCommerce plugin. `GET /v1/gas-sponsorships` also needs the `payments:read` scope.
- `PUT /v1/gas-policy` needs a secret key with the `sessions:write` scope. A connected site's key gets
  `403`: it can read the policy but not change it.
- In the dashboard, reading needs `account:read`. Changing the policy needs `settings:manage` and a
  recent step-up confirmation, because the policy decides how much of your money Tendr may spend.
- Publishable keys and shopper sessions are refused.

## The policy

```json
{
  "livemode": false,
  "enabled": true,
  "per_payment_cap_usd": "1",
  "monthly_budget_usd": "50",
  "min_order_usd": "0",
  "chains": ["base", "arb", "op", "pol", "bsc", "avax", "gnosis", "bera", "sol", "near", "tron"],
  "tokens": [],
  "first_time_only": false,
  "updated_at": "2026-10-27T09:00:00.000Z"
}
```

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `enabled` | Sponsorship is on. Default `false`. |
| `per_payment_cap_usd` | The quoted fee must be at most this. Default `"1"`. Never above the monthly budget. |
| `monthly_budget_usd` | Reserved plus spent per calendar month (UTC) never exceeds this. Default `"50"`. |
| `min_order_usd` | The order must be at least this. Default `"0"`. |
| `chains` | Where sponsorship may happen: any of `eth`, `base`, `arb`, `op`, `pol`, `bsc`, `avax`, `gnosis`, `bera`, `sol`, `near`, `tron`. Default is all except `eth`. |
| `tokens` | Allowed 1Click asset ids. Empty means any token. |
| `first_time_only` | Sponsor only the first payment from a shopper address to your store. Default `false`. |
| `updated_at` | Null while the defaults apply. |

USD amounts are decimal strings with at most 6 decimals.

`PUT` merges the fields you send over the current policy (or the defaults) and validates the result.
Unknown fields are rejected. A cap above the budget returns `400 VALIDATION_ERROR`.

```bash
curl -X PUT "$TENDR_API_URL/v1/gas-policy" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TENDR_SECRET_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "enabled": true, "monthly_budget_usd": "100", "chains": ["base", "sol"] }'
```

Every change is recorded in the audit log with who made it and the policy before and after.

The store's `default_gas_payer` and a session's `gas_payer` still decide whether Tendr tries to
sponsor at all. The policy decides whether a particular payment qualifies.

## Usage

```json
{
  "month": "2026-10",
  "reserved_usd": "0.8",
  "spent_usd": "12.3",
  "remaining_usd": "36.9",
  "count": 14
}
```

`reserved_usd` is held by sponsorships that have not settled yet. `spent_usd` is settled.
`remaining_usd` is the monthly budget minus both, never below zero. `count` is the number of
sponsorships holding budget. The month is a calendar month in UTC.

Budget is reserved when the shopper commits to sign, in one locked update. Two payments that together
would exceed the budget cannot both be sponsored: one is, the other falls back to shopper-pays. The
reservation is released if the payment is never submitted or fails.

## Sponsorship records

`GET /v1/gas-sponsorships` filters: `session`, `payment` (the payment's session), `status`
(`pending`, `submitted`, `confirmed`, `failed`), `month` (`YYYY-MM`, by creation time), `limit`
(1 to 100, default 50) and `starting_after` (a record id).

```json
{
  "gas_sponsorships": [
    {
      "id": "gas_...",
      "livemode": false,
      "session_id": "ses_...",
      "recovered_from_payment_id": null,
      "hook_payment_id": null,
      "chain": "base",
      "method": "eip3009",
      "provider": "relayer-evm",
      "sponsor": "merchant",
      "status": "confirmed",
      "tx_hash": "0x...",
      "user_op_hash": null,
      "fee_mode": null,
      "fee_asset": null,
      "fee_amount": null,
      "estimate_usd": "0.04",
      "cost_usd": "0.031",
      "gas_used": "88123",
      "reservation_state": "settled",
      "created_at": "2026-10-27T09:00:00.000Z",
      "updated_at": "2026-10-27T09:00:08.000Z"
    }
  ],
  "has_more": false,
  "next_cursor": null
}
```

- `sponsor` is `merchant` when the cost is taken from your payment, or `tendr` when Tendr could not
  recover it and absorbed the loss. Tendr never charges you later for a loss.
- `method` is one of `eip3009`, `permit`, `paymaster`, `kora`, `meta_tx`, `forwarder`. A hook call that costs
  more than its quoted estimate appears with `hook`; the excess is Tendr's loss (`sponsor: tendr`), and
  `hook_payment_id` names the payment whose hook it was. The stored method type also has
  `paymaster_verifying`, `paymaster_erc20`, `energy` and `fee_payer`; no record is written with them
  today, so you can treat them as reserved.
- `recovered_from_payment_id` is the payment whose proceeds paid a sponsored fee (set when its
  reservation is settled). It is null on every other record, hook shortfalls included.
- `estimate_usd` is the quoted cost your payment was charged. `cost_usd` is the actual cost. Tendr keeps
  or absorbs the difference. Both are shown on shopper-paid and Tendr-loss records too.
- `reservation_state` is `reserved`, `settled` or `released`, or null when nothing was reserved.
- `fee_mode`, `fee_asset` and `fee_amount` describe a shopper-paid fee (`fee_amount` in base units).
- Amounts are strings. `fee_amount` and `gas_used` are integers.

### A sponsored quote paid by copying the address

When you sponsor a quote, the network fee is part of the quote: it comes out of your proceeds through
1Click's fees on that quote. If the shopper then pays that quote by copying the deposit address and
sending from their own wallet (paying the gas themselves), the quote's fee is still taken from your
proceeds and counted against your budget, although Tendr paid no gas. This is a known difference from
the design, which says the shopper then simply pays their own fee. It is bounded by your per-payment cap
and monthly budget.

## Why a payment was not sponsored

When you sponsor and a payment falls back to the shopper, `network_fee.reason` on the session and
payment says why. The checks run in this order and the first failure is reported:

| `reason` | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `shopper_choice` | The resolved gas payer is `shopper`. |
| `policy_disabled` | `enabled` is false. |
| `chain` | The shopper's chain is not in `chains`. |
| `token` | The token is not in `tokens`. |
| `min_order` | The order is below `min_order_usd`. |
| `not_first_time` | `first_time_only` is set and this address paid your store before. |
| `per_payment_cap` | The fee is above the cap, above the session's `gas_max_usd`, or above 1% of the order (fee plus any hook estimate). |
| `loss_cap` | Tendr's daily unrecovered-loss limit was reached. |
| `budget` | The fee does not fit the month's remaining budget. |

Other reasons are `fee_unavailable` (the live fee could not be read, so the shopper's wallet pays gas
itself) and `fee_token_unpriced` (the token has no price in the token list, so the token option is not
offered). The shopper sees only a general message.

## Events

- `gas.budget_low`: reserved plus spent crossed 80% of the month's budget. At most once per month and
  mode.
- `gas.budget_exhausted`: a payment was not sponsored because the budget is used up. At most once per
  month and mode.

Both carry `mode`, `month`, `budget_usd`, `reserved_usd`, `spent_usd` and `remaining_usd`;
`gas.budget_exhausted` also carries `requested_usd`. See [webhooks](/api-reference/webhooks).
