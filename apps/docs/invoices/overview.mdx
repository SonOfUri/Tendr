title: 'Contractor invoices'
description: 'Receive itemized invoices and pay contractors from your Tendr wallet.'
---

An invoice requests an outgoing payment from your business. A payment link collects money from a customer.

Enable your page at `https://invoice.usetendr.tech/<business>` in invoice settings. You need an active native live wallet.

## Contractor workflow

1. Open the business invoice page.
2. Enter a name, email, reference and optional due date.
3. Add one to 50 breakdown lines with a description and amount in USD.
4. Select a supported receiving token/network and external wallet address.
5. Submit and verify the email using Tendr's link.

A full Tendr account is not required. Email verification makes the invoice available in the business inbox; it does not authorize payment.

## Exact totals

Line amounts and totals are in **US dollars**, with at most two decimal places. The backend stores and adds exact cents; choosing a receiving token does not change the dollar total.

For example, `$125.00` and `$25.50` make a **$150.50 invoice**, whether the contractor chooses USDC, ETH or another supported token. Submit the breakdown, not a separately editable total. Descriptions allow up to 200 characters. Memo/tag-dependent destinations are unsupported.

## Business workflow

Review contractor details, breakdown, reference, receiving asset and exact address. Select the store asset to fund payment, review the quote, then sign with the wallet passkey. Tendr fetches the receiving token's current USD price when preparing the payment quote and rounds its token output up to the next smallest unit. The quote fixes the token amount for approval; fees are added to the business's source-wallet debit. Token prices can change after approval; the token quantity is fixed for that payout. An expired quote may use a new rate, while the USD invoice total stays fixed. Tendr tracks delivery against the approved token amount before marking the invoice paid.

Previously issued token-denominated invoices keep their original amounts and remain explicitly labeled in their token.

| State                   | Meaning                      |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| `awaiting_verification` | Contractor must verify email |
| `open`                  | Ready for owner review       |
| `processing`            | Payment processing           |
| `paid`                  | Associated payout completed  |
| `rejected`              | Business declined            |
| `payment_review`        | Payment needs investigation  |

## Email and receipts

Contractors receive verification and status messages. Receipt links carry secret tokens; keep them private. The business receives a verified-invoice notification.

If verification expires, resubmit the same reference for a fresh link. Requests are rate-limited. Pausing incoming invoices does not cancel an already authorized payout.
