## Shared integration rules

Price orders on your server. Preserve the returned session URL and secret fragment. Confirm fulfillment only after verified `payment.succeeded` and server-side corroboration. There is no `payment.settled` webhook.

A dedicated store wallet authorizes outgoing funds. Installing a plugin does not grant it your wallet passkey.
