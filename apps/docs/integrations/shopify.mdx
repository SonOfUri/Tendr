<Warning>
  Tendr's Shopify connector is a pilot. It is not an approved, generally available Shopify payment
  method. It cannot currently be enabled in your Shopify checkout.
</Warning>

The pilot uses an offsite payment flow: Shopify starts a session, the shopper visits Tendr checkout, the connector verifies and reconciles the outcome, then reports it to Shopify. Refunds wait for Tendr wallet approval and confirmed execution.

## Production requirements

Shopify Payments Partner access and the applicable payments extension approval are required. Hosting must implement Shopify's payment security requirements, including authenticated payment endpoints and the required mutual TLS setup.

See Shopify's [offsite payments guide](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/payments/offsite/use-the-cli?framework=remix) and [payments extension review](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/payments/payments-extension-review).
