---
title: 'Login recovery'
description: 'Save recovery codes before losing access to your login passkeys.'
---

Tendr login recovery combines a saved recovery code with a fresh link sent to your verified email. It restores access to the same account and stores.

<Warning>
  Login recovery does not recover your dedicated wallet passkey. Sending funds still requires that
  wallet credential. Keep its synced or backed-up copy accessible.
</Warning>

## Set up recovery while you have access

1. Sign in and open **Login recovery** in your dashboard.
2. Optionally add a backup login passkey on another device or in another credential provider.
3. Choose **Generate recovery codes** and confirm with your current login passkey when asked.
4. Save the ten codes in a password manager or a secure printed backup you can reach without this device.
5. Confirm you saved them. The codes are shown only once.

Each code works once. Generating another set invalidates every code in the previous set and any pending recovery links. Tendr emails you when codes are generated.

## Recover after losing all login passkeys

1. On the sign-in page, select **Lost your login passkeys? Use a recovery code**.
2. Enter your account email and request a recovery link.
3. Open the email within 15 minutes and enter one saved code.
4. Choose **Replace lost login passkeys** and create a new passkey.
5. Sign in with the new passkey. Review your backup passkeys and remaining codes.

Recovery revokes your previous login passkeys and signs out existing sessions. One code is consumed, and a security notice is sent to your verified email. Your account identity, store memberships and wallet address stay the same.

## If you did not save codes

Try a synced copy of your passkey or another registered device first. Email sign-in can open the same account, but email alone cannot replace a missing login passkey or generate recovery codes. Recovery codes must be saved while you still have a working login passkey.

See [Wallets and passkeys](/wallets/overview) for the separate wallet signing requirements.
