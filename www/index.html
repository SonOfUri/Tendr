FAQ

Payments, answered.

What is Tendr Pay?

Tendr Pay helps businesses accept payments through one simple checkout, with support for different payment methods and digital assets.

How can my customers pay?

Customers can connect their wallet or pay with a supported asset. Tendr brings the payment experience together in one checkout, with supported assets and networks available through the platform.

How do I receive payments as a merchant?

You can choose from the supported settlement options available to your business. Tendr connects the checkout experience to the payment and settlement flow.

How can I integrate Tendr Pay into my business?

Tendr is designed to support checkout integrations into existing products and websites. Get in touch with our team to explore the integration options available for your business.

Do I need a developer?

Not necessarily. Tendr is designed to support different ways of accepting payments, from hosted checkout and payment links to developer integrations. You can use the options available for your business without building a checkout from scratch.
